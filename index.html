HOW TO PLAY

Cretagen's specimens are loose in Midtown and they're chasing your cab. Unit 77 drives itself — you ride the TAIL GUN, facing back down the avenue. Shoot them off your bumper, survive the combat zones, and put down the Apex when it comes for you.

AIM + HOLD LEFT CLICKFire the tail gun at whatever is under the crosshair. Headshots deal 1.6x; GRIDLOCK's glowing collar deals 2.5x.
RED RING = INCOMINGA dinosaur about to jump you glows red with a shrinking ring. Shoot it before the ring closes to STAGGER it out of the attack.
RRELOADThe belt reloads by itself when empty. R or right click reloads early.
SPACESKYHAMMERKills charge an air strike. It lands where the crosshair meets the street.
LEVEL UPFossilized dinosaurs drop gene cores. Pick 1 of 3 upgrades: support guns, passives, and EVOLUTIONS. Shoot floating supply drops to grab them.
TOUCHDrag anywhere to move the crosshair like a trackpad — your finger never covers it. While you hold, it fires whenever the crosshair is on a dinosaur (mild aim assist helps). Settings can switch to a crosshair that floats above your finger. RELOAD and SKYHAMMER buttons on the right.
