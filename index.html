HOW TO PLAY

Cretagen's specimens are loose in Midtown. Your armored cab rides the avenues — keep it alive, fossilize everything, survive until the Apex shows up… then put it down.

AIM + HOLD LEFT CLICKFire the Roof Cannon. The reticle snaps to targets; hit the T. rex's glowing collar for weak-point crits.
ADSTEERSlide across the avenue to dodge red lanes and ram small dinosaurs (they fly).
SHIFTNITROBoost out of stomp rings and plow through swarms. S brakes.
SPACESKYHAMMERKills charge an air strike. Call it onto the reticle. (Right click works too.)
GENE CORESFossilized dinosaurs drop cores. Level up, pick 1 of 3 upgrades. Max a weapon + own its partner passive to EVOLVE it.
TOUCHLeft thumb steers, the cannon auto-fires; tap the street to aim. NITRO and SKYHAMMER buttons on the right.
