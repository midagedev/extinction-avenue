Asset id: ptero — the "Skyreaver"  (assets/models/pterosaur.glb)

Original work of this project, generated in code by tools/lab/build-ptero.js (mesh, skeleton, skin weights) with
procedural animation in js/game/creature-clips.js. Released under CC0 1.0 Universal.

Why procedural: no CC0 rigged pterosaur in glTF exists in the sources searched (Quaternius, Poly Pizza, OpenGameArt,
Kenney, GitHub). The CC0 "Low Spec Pterosaur" (pistachio, OpenGameArt) is a 592-triangle .blend that can't be
converted without Blender; the CC0 Quaternius dragons/bat read as cartoon dragons, not pterosaurs.
