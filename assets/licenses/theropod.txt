Modifications by this project (released under CC0 1.0 as well):
  - Rigged: 27-bone skeleton placed by hand, skin weights computed by tools/lab/rig-theropod.js (nearest-segment,
    neighbour-limited blending, geodesic jaw/skull split); 6.7k-triangle LOD1 generated with three.js
    SimplifyModifier. The source mesh shipped with no rig and no animation.
  - Animation: procedural keyframes generated at runtime (js/game/creature-clips.js).
  - Textures: the original 2048² colour bake and normal map re-encoded as JPEG (q86 / q90), no other change.
  - Species variants (Scuttler, Sicklejaw, Brute, GRIDLOCK) are runtime bone-scale morphs + shader recolours.
