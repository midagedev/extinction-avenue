Why a stegosaurus: no CC0 rigged/animated ankylosaur could be found (the only CC0 ankylosaur on OpenGameArt is an
unrigged 1.8k-triangle FBX/.blend). The armoured slot reuses the closest good CC0 model.

Modifications by this project (CC0 1.0): crease-aware Loop subdivision x2 (2.3k -> 36k triangles, LOD1 9k) with
interpolated skin weights and recoloured materials (tools/lab/refine-quaternius.js). Skeleton and clips unchanged.
